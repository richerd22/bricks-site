About

Off the Bricks is a set of walking hunts through Boston. Every fact on a story card has a source you can open, and when a famous story doesn't hold up, we tell you the legend first and then what the record says.

A Third Polar Arch project, made in Boston. Not affiliated with the City of Boston, the MBTA, Massport, or any institution named in a pack. Nothing in this app asks you to touch, open or tape anything to a fire alarm box. Look, don't touch.
