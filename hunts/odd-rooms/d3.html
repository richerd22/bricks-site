Odd Rooms · DETOUR · Dorchester

The Museum of Bad Art started with a painting a dealer pulled out of the trash because he wanted the frame.

An antique dealer pulled a painting out of the trash on a Roslindale sidewalk because he wanted the frame. His friends wanted the painting. That was the start of the Museum of Bad Art, which spent years in theater basements, once just outside a men's room, and now hangs in a taproom on Mass Ave in Dorchester. It's free, and it's open late.
