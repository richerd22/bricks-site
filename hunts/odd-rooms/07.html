Odd Rooms · STOP 07 · Back Bay

In the basement of this Newbury Street church, anyone can borrow a twelve-foot dragon for free, and they sign a notebook to do it.

In the basement of a Back Bay church, through a door in the alley, is a free lending library of handmade puppets, some twelve feet tall: dragons that need up to a dozen people. You borrow one by writing your name in a composition notebook. 'They always bring them back,' its maker told the Harvard Crimson. Posted hours and house rules apply. The stop is the church's Newbury Street front.

This is stop 7 of Odd Rooms. Walk it - any two stops are free.
