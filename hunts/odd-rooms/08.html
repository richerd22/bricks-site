Odd Rooms · STOP 08 · Back Bay

The state took the genealogists' building for its own offices, so they bought a bank on Newbury Street and stacked five floors on it.

In the 1960s the Commonwealth took the genealogists' building near the State House for its own offices, and paid less than the appraisal, per the society's own history. So the country's oldest genealogical society bought a 1928 bank branch on Newbury Street and, by its own account, stacked five floors on top. The door is in the building next door. The bank is still underneath.

This is stop 8 of Odd Rooms. Walk it - any two stops are free.
