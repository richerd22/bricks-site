Invented Here · STOP 07 · Theatre District / Downtown Crossing

The Boston Athenaeum calls the Bijou the first theater in the United States lit entirely by electricity. London's Savoy had already lit a stage.

In December 1882 a narrow theater on Washington Street opened Gilbert and Sullivan's Iolanthe under more than six hundred Edison lamps, and the Boston Athenaeum says Edison supervised the wiring himself. It gets called the first theater in the United States lit entirely by electricity. London got there first. Most of the building came down in 2008; the front is still standing, next to a much bigger sign.
