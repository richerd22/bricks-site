Made Land · DETOUR · Downtown

In 1996 an artist carved Boston's 1630 high-tide line into this plaza, and left no sign saying so.

The paving around the statue in Dock Square, beside Faneuil Hall, carries a line: Boston's high-tide shoreline in about 1630, sandblasted in 1996 through stencils of seaweed, skate egg cases, rope and feathers the artist collected on real high-tide lines. There is no sign. That's deliberate. Everything downhill of the line used to be harbor. Go find the edge.
