Shot Here · STOP 11 · Faneuil Hall Marketplace

For nineteen years this marketplace had a copy of a TV set of a Boston bar.

For nineteen years Faneuil Hall Marketplace had a bar that copied a TV set, which was itself loosely based on a real Boston pub. The Cheers replica poured its last on August 30, 2020. The market had a film career before that, in The Verdict (1982). The gold letters across Quincy Market's portico look original. They went up in 1989. Look up and decide what else is.
