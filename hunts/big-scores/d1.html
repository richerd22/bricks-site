Big Scores · DETOUR · Fenway

Thirteen works were taken from the Gardner in 1990, and the empty frames still hang where they were.

In the early hours of March 18, 1990, two men dressed as police talked their way into the Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum and left 81 minutes later with thirteen works, among them a Vermeer and a Rembrandt. None has come back. Gardner's will said the galleries couldn't be changed, so the empty frames still hang. Outside, her motto is still carved over the old front door.
