The 1852 Hunt · STOP 02 · Downtown Crossing

Boston's first keyless alarm box went in on this corner in 1881.

For its first thirty years you couldn't just pull a Boston fire alarm. The boxes were locked and the keys went to trusted citizens - until the department changed every lock because, its own history says, the keys were held by 'irresponsible people.' The first keyless box door in the city went in on one corner across from the Common in 1881. There's still a box on that corner.
