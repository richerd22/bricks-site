The 1852 Hunt · DETOUR · Fenway

Every fire alarm box in Boston has reported to this building on the Fens since 8 a.m., December 27, 1925.

Every street fire alarm box in Boston reports to one limestone building on the Back Bay Fens, and has since December 1925. It took a special act of the Legislature to put it on the parkland. The inscription across its front says exactly what it was built to fight. Read it from the park path.
