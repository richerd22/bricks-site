Loud Boston · DETOUR · Allston

Aerosmith lived on the second floor of 1325 Commonwealth Avenue in the 1970s.

In the 1970s Aerosmith shared the second floor of an apartment building on Comm Ave in Allston, and in 2012 the band came back for a free noon show out front and a City plaque. Steven Tyler has said they recorded 'Dream On' in the basement. People still live here, so it's a sidewalk stop: read the plaque, take the picture, leave the door clear.
