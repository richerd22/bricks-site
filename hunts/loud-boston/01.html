Loud Boston · STOP 01 · South End

Lou Reed called this old church the Velvet Underground's 'favorite place to play in the whole country.'

From January 1967 an old church at Berkeley and Appleton in the South End kept rock-club hours as the Boston Tea Party. Lou Reed called it the Velvet Underground's favorite place to play in the whole country, and Led Zeppelin played it in 1969. Something in its front window has fooled people for years about what the building was for. The marker by the door sets it straight.
