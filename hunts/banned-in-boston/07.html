Banned in Boston · STOP 07 · Chinatown / old Combat Zone

When Scollay Square was razed, its trade moved here, and in November 1974 Boston made it official.

In November 1974 Boston stopped trying to ban its red-light district and zoned it instead: two blocks of lower Washington Street, and nowhere else. The planners' first improvement was a new name borrowed from the Revolution. A small plaza at Boylston and Washington carries it now, and the building across the street is worth looking up at.
