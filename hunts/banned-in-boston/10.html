Banned in Boston · STOP 10 · Theater District

Groucho Marx, playing here in October 1929, gave Boston's censorship a one-line review: 'Boston's a pain in the neck.'

In October 1929, with Boston's mayor banning plays and Margaret Sanger, Groucho Marx was on stage at this Tremont Street theatre and told a Harvard Crimson reporter what he thought of the city. The marker Boston put out front has a small problem of its own. Read it slowly, then look up at the marquee.
