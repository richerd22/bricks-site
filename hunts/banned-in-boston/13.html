Banned in Boston · STOP 13 · Beacon Hill

The Censors' Last Address

The Watch and Ward Society spent decades telling Boston what not to read. In 1933 it moved its headquarters to Beacon Hill. Decades later the same building belonged to the people whose press published the Society's history, under the title Banned in Boston. It's a private building on Mount Vernon Street; look from the sidewalk.
