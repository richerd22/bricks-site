Bad Ends · DETOUR · Longwood Medical Area

The surgeon from the ether operation left Harvard his museum and his skeleton. The star exhibit is a skull with a hole in it.

John Collins Warren, the surgeon from the first public ether operation, gave Harvard his teaching collection in 1847 and later left it his own skeleton. Its most famous object arrived in 1868: the skull of Phineas Gage, with the iron bar that went through it in 1848. He lived, and who he became afterward is still argued over. The weekend gallery upstairs shows his case. Check the hours first.
