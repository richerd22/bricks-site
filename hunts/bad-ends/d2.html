Bad Ends · DETOUR · South End

A 1911 paper put Boston's Quaker gallows out here on the Neck, using a judge's diary and a 1722 map.

Out here on Washington Street, Boston was once a thin neck of land with one road and a gate across it. A 1911 paper for the Bostonian Society argued the colony's gallows stood just outside that gate, and that Mary Dyer and the other hanged Quakers died out here, not on the Common. Its evidence: a judge's diary and a 1722 map. Nothing marks it. Look north.
