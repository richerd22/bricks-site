Bad Ends · STOP 08 · Beacon Hill

The plinth says she was hanged on the Common. A judge's diary points a mile south.

Mary Dyer was already standing on the ladder once when the colony let her go. She came back, and Boston hanged her. Her bronze statue sits outside the State House, and its plinth says she was hanged on Boston Common. A 1911 paper, working from a judge's diary and an old map, put the gallows a mile south. Read the plinth and pick a side.
