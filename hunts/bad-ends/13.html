Bad Ends · STOP 13 · Public Garden

Boston built a monument to anesthesia and left every name off it.

Boston built a tall monument in the Public Garden to the discovery of ether anesthesia and left every discoverer's name off it. The dentist who held the inhaler and the physician who shared his patent were fighting over the credit, so the stone credits the Bible and a Good Samaritan in a turban instead. Walk all the way around it and look for a name.

This is stop 13 of Bad Ends. Walk it - any two stops are free.
