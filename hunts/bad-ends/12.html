Cocoanut Grove, 1942

The Cocoanut Grove nightclub stood on this block of Bay Village. On the night of November 28, 1942, a fire killed hundreds of people inside. The main entrance was a revolving door, and other exits were locked or jammed. Fire and building codes across the country changed because of it. A bronze plaque made by a survivor, the club's youngest employee, is set in the sidewalk.

Northeast corner of Piedmont Street and Cocoanut Grove Lane (formerly Shawmut Street Extension), Bay Village. Moved in 2016 from in front of 17 Piedmont Street.
