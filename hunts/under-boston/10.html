Under Boston · STOP 10 · Leather District / Chinatown

The state and the plant's own owner have both drawn up plans to tear this steam plant down. It's still here.

A brick plant on Kneeland Street has been making steam for the pipes under downtown since 1929. In 2016 the state offered the site with a plan to tear the plant down and rebuild it. Nobody bid. In 2021 its own owner proposed replacing it with apartments and labs. It's still there.

This is stop 10 of Under Boston. Walk it - any two stops are free.
