Under Boston · DETOUR · Dorchester (Columbia Point)

On New Year's Day 1884 this building started lifting Boston's sewage about 35 feet and sending it under the harbor.

From New Year's Day 1884, Boston's sewage went to a granite castle on Columbia Point to be lifted about thirty-five feet and sent through a tunnel under the harbor. Its steam engines had flywheels of 72.5 tons apiece, and its architect also restored the Old State House. Take the Red Line three stops past South Station to JFK/UMass and walk toward the water.
