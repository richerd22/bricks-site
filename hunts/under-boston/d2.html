Under Boston · DETOUR · Downtown / Boston Common

The first American subway car reached Park Street three minutes after it entered the tunnel, and this entrance has been collecting plaques ever since.

On September 1, 1897, the first car on America's first subway pulled into Park Street three minutes after it entered the tunnel at the Public Garden. Since then, three separate institutions have bolted plaques to this entrance to say it matters. Read them on your way to the train.
